## Cold outreach method (current)
- **Volume:** 100/day per channel (email, calls, DMs), for at least 100 days. Math: ~100 reach-outs ≈ 1 client.
- **Lead magnet / opener:** the free **Win-Back Campaign** — "Send me your old customer list. I'll text them a holiday detail offer this week, for free. Any cars that book are 100% yours." Then: "Want 10 more new customers a month like that? You only pay when they show up."
- **Rules:** personalize with 1–3 real facts about the shop; 3rd-grade reading level; never mention prices, fake urgency, or made-up results in cold outreach.
- **Sequence:** Day 1 email 1 + call; Day 2 call + IG DM with voice memo; Day 4 email 2; Day 7 call + DM; Day 11 email 3 (seasonal idea); Day 18 break-up email; +90 days restart with new angle. Email goes first so calls/DMs can say "following up on my email."
- **Automation:** lead finder runs 6:41am + 12:41pm ET weekdays (~100 detailers/day); email sender hourly 8am–5pm ET (30/day now, ramping to 80 by late Nov); inbox sync 10:23am + 4:23pm ET. Juan does 100 calls (afternoon) + 100 DMs w/ voice memo (morning) and texts Claude one line at end of day to log them.
- **Targets:** email 30% open / 3% reply; calls 20% pickup / 5% yes; DMs 50% seen / 20% reply. 1 in 3 engaged → opportunity, 1 in 3 opportunities → client.
