# SELLO Automation Package

Copy these into each client's setup. Replace [Business], [Name], [Link].

## Free way to run it (no CRM bill)
- **Booking + deposits + reminders:** Square Appointments free plan (online booking, prepayment/no-show protection, automatic text/email reminders), or the SELLO booking page + a free Stripe Payment Link.
- **Instant replies on IG/FB:** Meta Business Suite > Inbox > Automations (free): instant reply + FAQ replies.
- **Missed-call text-back:** Google Voice (free) or the phone's auto-reply; upgrade to a paid tool later.
- **Follow-ups, rebook and review texts:** schedule from the SELLO CRM list until revenue pays for a CRM (then move these templates into it).

## 1. Instant reply (under 1 minute)
"Hey [Name], it's [Business]! Thanks for reaching out. What car do you have, and which service are you looking for? You can grab a time here: [Link]"

## 2. Missed-call text-back
"Sorry I missed you, I'm finishing up a car right now. What can I help you with? You can also book here: [Link]"

## 3. Follow-ups (no booking yet)
- **Day 1:** "Hey [Name], still want to get your car looking brand new? I have a couple of spots open this week: [Link]"
- **Day 3:** "Quick one, [Name]: most of our customers go with the Full Detail. Want me to hold a spot for you?"
- **Day 7:** "Last check-in, [Name]. Should I close out your request, or do you still want that detail?"

## 4. Booking confirmation
"You're booked, [Name]! [Service] on [Date] at [Time]. Your $[Deposit] deposit comes off your total. Reply here if anything changes."

## 5. Reminders
- **24 hours before:** "Reminder: your detail is tomorrow at [Time]. Please remove valuables from the car. See you then!"
- **On the way:** "On my way! Be there in about [X] minutes."

## 6. Job done + review request (2 hours after)
"Thanks for trusting [Business] with your car, [Name]! How did it turn out? If you loved it, a quick review helps a ton: [Review link]"
- If they're unhappy: "I'm sorry to hear that. Call me directly at [Phone] and I'll make it right."

## 7. Rebook texts
- **Day 30:** "Hey [Name], it's been a month. Time for a maintenance detail to keep that shine? [Link]"
- **Day 60:** "Your car's probably due, [Name]. Want me to hold a spot this week?"
- **Day 90:** "Haven't seen you in a while! Here's $20 off your next detail this week only: [Link]"

## 8. Win-Back Campaign (old customer list, send once)
"Hey [Name], it's [Business]! It's been a while since we did your car. This week only: $30 off a full detail. Reply YES and I'll send you times."

## 9. Friday results text (to the detailer, from SELLO)
"Week recap: [X] leads, [Y] booked, [Z] showed up. Ad spend $[A], cost per show $[B]. Next week: [one action]."
