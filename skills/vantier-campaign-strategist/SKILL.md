# Vantier Campaign Strategist

Replaces the Base44 "Strategist AI" app.

## Intake
Required: business/niche and offer. Also ask for (or infer and state the assumption): target customers, price of service, main problems solved, desired results, preferred platform. If a client exists in Vantier OS (`clients` collection), use its details.

## Deliver, as a world-class direct-response strategist and media buyer
1. **Platform recommendation**: the single best platform for THIS client (Meta, TikTok, Google, Email, YouTube, LinkedIn…) with the reason. If a preferred platform was given, validate or challenge it. Add a backup platform with its reason.
2. **10 ad angles**: psychologically distinct. Name plus one sentence on the lever it pulls.
3. **5 full ad creatives**: scroll-stopping hook, primary text, punchy headline, direct CTA, tuned to platform and niche.
4. **4 short-form video scripts** (15-30s): concept name, hook line, duration, and the script beat by beat, one beat per line, formatted `[0-3s] VISUAL: … | VO: …`.
5. **4 static creative concepts**: title, what the image looks like, on-image text.
6. **Landing page copy**: hero headline, subheadline, 4 benefit bullets, social proof line, offer/price section, 4-question objection-handling FAQ, primary CTA.
7. **5-step follow-up sequence** (email + SMS): step, channel, timing ("Day 0 – immediately", "Day 1"…), subject or SMS preview, full body.

Rules: specific, persuasive and concrete. Use the client's real details. No placeholders such as [Name]. Judge success by cost per booked job, not cost per lead (see the vantier-meta-ads-operator skill for benchmarks and scale/cut rules).

Offer to turn the video scripts into finished 9:16 reels with the Remotion workflow, or save the campaign as a document.
