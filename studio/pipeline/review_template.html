Status

Every piece is a Draft. Approve in Vantier OS (Content) or your own checklist before scheduling. Files, captions and scripts live in the repository under vantier-instagram-library/.

Visuals

Stock libraries were unreachable from the production environment, so the Reels use a custom motion system instead: line-art illustrations for each trade, phone and ad mockups, kinetic type and animated data, all timed to the narration. STOCK_SHOTLIST.md names an optional licensed clip for every scene, by timecode.

Check before posting

  • R01, R09, R20 and R25 include AI photo stills made on the ElevenLabs free plan. Confirm commercial rights or swap them for stock.
  • All numbers and the review in R21 are labeled examples. No client results are claimed.

Voice

Narration uses Kokoro TTS (Apache-2.0, commercially safe) in one consistent voice, because the ElevenLabs account had too few credits left for 34 Reels. The biggest upgrade is re-voicing in Juan's own voice; the studio re-times captions and re-renders automatically.

Audio

Original Vantier music beds (Pulse, Glass, Drive), fully owned. Subtle sound design. Every Reel is mastered to -14 LUFS. Each Reel also ships a voice + SFX stem without music, for adding a trending sound in the app.

Never targeted

Restaurants, med spas, dentists, chiropractors, law firms, accounting firms and insurance agencies. An automated scan of every script, caption and slide confirms none appear. No piece depends on one city.
