How posting works

Approve a post and the Instagram publisher sends it to @official.vantier at its scheduled time. It runs at 10:50 AM, 1:50 PM and 6:50 PM Eastern and posts one approved post per run. Back to draft holds a post; Reject parks it; Delete removes it from the queue. The publisher's on/off switch and run log stay in Vantier OS → Automations.

Timing rules

  • A post goes out on the first run after its scheduled time.
  • Two posts in the same window go out one run apart.
  • A post that misses its time by more than 6 hours is marked failed instead of posting late. Reschedule and re-approve it.

Visuals

Stock libraries were unreachable from the production environment, so the library Reels use a custom motion system: line-art illustrations for each trade, phone and ad mockups, kinetic type and animated data, all timed to the narration. STOCK_SHOTLIST.md names an optional licensed clip for every scene, by timecode.

Check before approving

  • R01, R09, R20 and R25 include AI photo stills made on the ElevenLabs free plan. Confirm commercial rights or swap them for stock.
  • All numbers and the review in R21 are labeled examples. No client results are claimed.

Voice and audio

Narration uses Kokoro TTS (Apache-2.0, commercially safe) in one consistent voice. Re-voicing in Juan's own voice is the biggest upgrade; the studio re-times captions and re-renders automatically. Music beds are original and fully owned, mastered to -14 LUFS.

Never targeted

Restaurants, med spas, dentists, chiropractors, law firms, accounting firms and insurance agencies. An automated scan of every library script, caption and slide confirms none appear. No piece depends on one city.
