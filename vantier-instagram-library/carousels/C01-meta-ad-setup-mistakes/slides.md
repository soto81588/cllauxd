## Slides

1. **cover** — 5 Meta ad mistakes quietly draining your budget
2. **point** — Picking the wrong objective — Engagement and traffic campaigns find people who like and click, not people who book. If you want calls and form fills, choose Leads.
3. **point** — No tracking set up — Without the Meta pixel and Conversions API, Meta can't learn who actually becomes a customer. You're optimizing blind.
4. **point** — Splitting a small budget five ways — Five ad sets at $10 a day means none of them get enough data to learn. Consolidate into fewer ad sets.
5. **point** — Judging results after two days — Early delivery is noisy. Give a new ad 5–7 days and enough spend before you decide.
6. **point** — Paying for clicks you can't serve — Check your map. Tighten the radius and exclude the zip codes you don't work in. Clicks from 40 miles away are a tax on your budget.
7. **statement** — Fix these five and results often improve without spending a dollar more.
8. **final** — Save this before your next campaign.
