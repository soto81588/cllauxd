## Slides

1. **cover** — Anatomy of an ad that books jobs
2. **ad** — THE FULL AD
3. **ad** — 1 — THE HOOK — Calls out the exact customer in the first line. Not your company name.
4. **ad** — 2 — THE PROBLEM — Names what they're worried about, in their words: leaks, surprise bills, contractors who vanish.
5. **ad** — 3 — THE OFFER — A specific, low-risk next step. Not a $15,000 decision.
6. **ad** — 4 — THE PROOF — A number or a name. Specific beats “trusted and reliable.”
7. **ad** — 5 — THE CTA — One action, plus what happens next and how fast.
8. **final** — Want us to tear down your current ad?
