## Slides

1. **cover** — What to post this week if you run a service business
2. **day** — Job of the week — A before-and-after, with the price range and how long it took.
3. **day** — Answer a real question — The question customers ask you on every call. Answer it on camera in 30 seconds.
4. **day** — Meet the crew — 15 seconds with one team member. People hire people.
5. **day** — Review spotlight — Read a real review on camera, over footage of that job.
6. **day** — Behind the scenes — The part of the job customers never see, and why you do it right.
7. **day** — Open slots — “3 openings next week.” A simple reminder with your booking link.
8. **statement** — 6 posts. 1 hour of filming. All from your phone.
9. **final** — Save this as your weekly checklist.

