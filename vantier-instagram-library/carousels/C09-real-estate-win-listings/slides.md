## Slides

1. **cover** — Agents: use every listing to win the next one
2. **statement** — Every listing ad is seen by neighbors. Neighbors are future sellers.
3. **point** — Target the surrounding zip codes — Run your “Just Listed” and “Just Sold” content to the neighborhood, not just to buyers.
4. **point** — Use the hook sellers care about — “Homes on your street are going under contract fast. Curious what yours is worth?”
5. **point** — Offer a no-pressure next step — A free home value report beats “call me” for someone who isn't ready to talk yet.
6. **point** — Follow up with the street, not a newsletter — Hyper-local market updates (“3 homes sold on Maple this month”) keep you top of mind.
7. **statement** — One listing, marketed right, can become your next three.
8. **final** — Share this with an agent who wants more listings.
