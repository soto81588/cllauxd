## Slides

1. **cover** — 6 ad formats that work for local service businesses
2. **point** — Owner to camera — 30 seconds, you on camera, talking to one customer. Trust builds faster with a face.
3. **point** — Before & after — Problem first, transformation second, proof last. Under 15 seconds.
4. **point** — Review read — A real review on screen, over footage of that exact job.
5. **point** — POV job footage — First-person clips of the work. Raw beats polished here.
6. **point** — Myth-buster — “You don't need a whole new roof.” Teach something, earn trust.
7. **point** — Day in the life — Crew, trucks, coffee, jobs. Shows you're real and established.
8. **statement** — Test 2–3 formats at once. Let booked jobs, not likes, pick the winner.
9. **final** — Which format are you trying first? Comment below.
