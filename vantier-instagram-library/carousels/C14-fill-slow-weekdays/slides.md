2. **point** — Rebook before they leave — “Same time in 3 weeks?” The easiest booking you'll ever get.
3. **point** — Create a weekday-only service — Express cuts, gloss treatments, beard tune-ups. Priced to move, only Tue–Thu.
4. **point** — Start a last-minute list — Clients who want early notice. Text it the moment someone cancels.
5. **point** — Run a small slow-day ad — Only on your slow days, only to people within a few miles.
6. **point** — Reward weekday regulars — Every 5th weekday visit, an add-on on the house.

Saturdays fill themselves. Tuesdays are where salons and barbershops win or lose.

Five ways to fill your chairs on slow weekdays:

Your busy days take care of themselves. Your slow days are where the extra profit is.
