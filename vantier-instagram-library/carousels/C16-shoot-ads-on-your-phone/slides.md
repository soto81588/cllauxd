## Slides

1. **cover** — Your phone is all you need to make great ads
2. **point** — Shoot vertical. Always. — 9:16 fills the whole screen on Reels and Stories.
3. **point** — Face the light — Window or sun in front of you, never behind you.
4. **point** — Open on action, not a greeting — Skip “Hey guys, it's Mike from…” Start with the problem.
5. **point** — Get close to the mic — Bad audio kills more ads than bad video. Stand near the phone or use a $20 clip-on mic.
6. **point** — Film 10 clips per job — Before, during, after, the crew, the customer's reaction.
7. **point** — Keep it real — Polished commercials get scrolled. Real job sites get watched.
8. **final** — Save this for your next job.
