## Hook (first 2 seconds)

## Voice-over script

Your business doesn't have a lead problem. It has a what-happens-next problem. Think about the last ten people who called you. How many hit voicemail while you were under a sink? How many got a callback the next morning? By then, they'd already booked someone else. More ads won't fix that. They just pour water into a leaky bucket. Fix the bucket first. Answer fast, text back in minutes, and follow up. Then turn the ads up. Follow Vantier for marketing that books jobs, not just calls.
