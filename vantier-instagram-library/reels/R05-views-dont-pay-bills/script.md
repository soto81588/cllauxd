## Hook (first 2 seconds)
- **On screen:** Big number: 50,000 — views (0 bookings)
- **Spoken:** “Fifty thousand views. Zero bookings.”

## Voice-over script

Fifty thousand views. Zero bookings. A detailing video blows up, and the calendar doesn't move. Because views measure attention, not intent. Attention is watching a car get polished. Intent is asking how much for yours. So track the numbers that show intent. Price DMs. Clicks to your booking page. Slots filled this week. Two thousand views with six booking requests beats fifty thousand with none. Every time. Save this, and check your numbers this week.

## Sound design
Cut whooshes on scene changes, UI pops/dings on phone messages, ticks on data reveals, a low impact on the hook and big numbers. Music ducks about 6 dB under the voice. Master at -14 LUFS with a -1.5 dBFS peak ceiling.

## Trending-audio option
`audio-stem_vo-sfx_no-music.m4a` is the voice + SFX without music. To use a trending sound, post the Reel, add the sound in Instagram at about 8–12% volume and lower original audio to 100%. Look for a soft lo-fi or felt-piano instrumental.
