Retargeting keeps you in front of them while they decide. Day one, they see your crew at work. Day four, a review from someone nearby. Day ten, a reason to book this month. ## Sound design
Cut whooshes on scene changes, UI pops/dings on phone messages, ticks on data reveals, a low impact on the hook and big numbers. Music ducks about 6 dB under the voice. Master at -14 LUFS with a -1.5 dBFS peak ceiling.

## Trending-audio option
`audio-stem_vo-sfx_no-music.m4a` is the voice + SFX without music. To use a trending sound, post the Reel, add the sound in Instagram at about 8–12% volume and lower original audio to 100%. Look for a soft lo-fi or felt-piano instrumental.
