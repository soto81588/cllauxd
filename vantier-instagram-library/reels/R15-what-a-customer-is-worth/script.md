## Voice-over script

That hundred-fifty-dollar customer isn't worth a hundred fifty dollars. Say you run a pest control company. A new customer signs up for quarterly service at a hundred fifty a visit. If they stay three years, that's eighteen hundred. Plus the neighbor they refer. So when you ask, can I afford sixty dollars to get a customer, you're asking the wrong question. Know what a customer is worth over time, and you'll stop being scared to invest in getting one. Save this and run your own numbers.
