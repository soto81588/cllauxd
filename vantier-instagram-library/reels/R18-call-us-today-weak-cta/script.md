## Voice-over script

Call us today might be the weakest line in your ad. Calling feels like work, especially for someone booking a cleaning on their lunch break. They don't know what to say, what it costs, or how long it'll take. So make the first step smaller. Text a photo of your space, get a price in ten minutes. Pick a time online in thirty seconds. Answer three quick questions, get an instant quote. The easier the first step, the more people take it. Comment your current call to action, and we'll make it stronger.
