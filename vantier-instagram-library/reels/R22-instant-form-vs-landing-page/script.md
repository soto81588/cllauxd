## Hook (first 2 seconds)

## Voice-over script

Instant form or landing page? Here's the honest answer. Instant forms are fast. People never leave Instagram. That means more leads, and more people who forgot they filled it out. Landing pages take more effort, so fewer finish. But they're usually more serious. For most local service businesses, start with an instant form. Just build it right. Use the higher intent form type, so people review their info before submitting. Add one qualifying question, like timeline or zip code. Then call while they still remember you. Save this before you build your next campaign.
