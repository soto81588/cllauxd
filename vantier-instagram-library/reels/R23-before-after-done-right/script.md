## Voice-over script

Before and after videos work. Most people just film them backwards. They lead with the finished result. But the before is the hook. Open on the worst of it. The black-streaked driveway. The dog hair in the back seat. Hold it just long enough to make people wince. Then the reveal, in one satisfying pass. End with the reaction, the price, or how fast you did it. Problem. Transformation. Proof. Under fifteen seconds. Send this to someone who films their own content.
