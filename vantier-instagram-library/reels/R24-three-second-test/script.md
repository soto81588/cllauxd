## Voice-over script

Try the three-second test on your Instagram. Hand your phone to a stranger. Open your profile. Count to three. Can they tell what you do? Where you do it? And why they should pick you? If your bio says, living the dream, established two thousand nine, that's a no. Try something like this instead. Your profile isn't a scrapbook. It's a storefront. Do the test, and comment your score out of three.

## Sound design
Cut whooshes on scene changes, UI pops/dings on phone messages, ticks on data reveals, a low impact on the hook and big numbers. Music ducks about 6 dB under the voice. Master at -14 LUFS with a -1.5 dBFS peak ceiling.

## Trending-audio option
`audio-stem_vo-sfx_no-music.m4a` is the voice + SFX without music. To use a trending sound, post the Reel, add the sound in Instagram at about 8–12% volume and lower original audio to 100%. Look for a slow-building, minimal tension track (dark ambient / cinematic pulse).
