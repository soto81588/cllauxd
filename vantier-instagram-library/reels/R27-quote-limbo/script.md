## Voice-over script

You sent the quote, and never heard back. Most owners take that as a no. It usually isn't. On a big project like a kitchen remodel, silence means busy, overwhelmed, or comparing. The contractor who follows up well usually wins. Day two, call. Don't email. Any questions on the numbers? Day five, send a photo of a similar project you just finished. Day ten. We're planning next month's schedule. Want me to hold a spot? Some of your best jobs are sitting in quotes you already sent. Save this, and follow up on one quote today.
