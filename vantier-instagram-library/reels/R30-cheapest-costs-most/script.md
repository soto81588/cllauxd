## Voice-over script

Being the cheapest option is costing you more than you think. Low prices attract customers who only care about price. They haggle. They cancel. They leave one star over a missed corner. And they're gone the second someone's ten dollars cheaper. The customers you actually want are shopping for something else. Reliability. Communication. Not having to think about it.
