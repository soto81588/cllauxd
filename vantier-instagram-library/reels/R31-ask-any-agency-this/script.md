## Voice-over script

Before you hire any marketing agency, ask them this. How will we know if this is working? If the answer is impressions, reach, or engagement, keep walking. You can't deposit impressions. The right answer is leads, how many you reached, how many booked, and what each booked job cost you. Then ask, who owns the ad account? And can I see the numbers anytime? The answers should be you, and yes. That goes for us too. Save this for the next agency that calls.
