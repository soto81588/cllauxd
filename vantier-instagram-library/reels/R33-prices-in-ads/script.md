## Voice-over script

Should you put prices in your ads? Most owners say no. They're scared of scaring people off. But that's kind of the point. A starting-at price filters out people who were never going to pay it. The ones who still reach out already know your range. Fewer leads, but better ones. And fewer awkward phone calls. You don't need a full price list. A from price or a typical range is enough. Transparency reads as confidence. Confidence reads as quality. Comment yes or no. Do you show prices?
